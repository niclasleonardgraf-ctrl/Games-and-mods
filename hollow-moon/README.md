# Hollow Moon

An action RPG set in Mystic Falls. You step off the bus alive and human. What you become after that is up to you, and nothing in the game decides it for you.

Unofficial fan game set in the world of *The Vampire Diaries*, with original characters. Not affiliated with the series. The names are ours; the rules for witches, werewolves, vampires, hybrids and Originals are the show's.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser (it loads three.js from a CDN, so you need to be online). Keyboard and mouse, or touch.

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD | left thumb |
| Attack (hold for a heavy blow) | left mouse / J (K for the heavy blow) | ATTACK |
| Block; tap just in time to parry | right mouse / Shift | BLOCK |
| Dodge | Space | DODGE |
| Ranged shot | F | SHOOT |
| Abilities | 1 2 3 4 | the round buttons |
| Heal | R | ✚ |
| Talk, open, gather, feed, finish what is down | E | USE |
| Pull out a wooden bolt lodged in you | hold E | hold USE |
| Change shape (a hybrid, or a werewolf wearing a moonlight ring on the full moon) | G | SHIFT |
| Pack, skills, journal, codex, map | Tab (M for map) | Menu |

As a wolf you bite (attack) and pounce (heavy). Weapons, shots, blocking, spells, draughts and conversation need hands.

## Who you are

You choose a bloodline on the title screen. It is what you were born with and it never changes. Whichever you choose, you arrive human.

- **Human.** An ordinary Fell. Only an ordinary human can carry the mark of the Brotherhood of the Five (the last skill of the Hunter tree: nobody can compel you, and the mark grows with every vampire you kill), and a Founder's Ring only brings back someone like you.
- **Witch.** Thorne blood on your mother's side. The craft is asleep until Odette Thorne wakes it. Spells cost focus; cast with too little and it comes out of your health, starting with the nose. It belongs to the living: a witch who becomes a vampire loses her magic for good, and the points come back.
- **Siphoner.** You make no magic of your own. You take it by touch from anything that has some: vampires, werewolves, witches, spirits, a ward, the altar at Coven Hollow. If you turn you keep it, as a Heretic, and your spells feed on your own blood.
- **Werewolf gene.** Lockwood blood on your father's side. The curse is asleep and wakes the first time you take a human life, so you pull your blows: people you beat are knocked out, not killed, unless you stand over one and choose to finish them.

## What you can become

Every change is a decision, and the game asks before the ones that cannot be undone.

- **Vampire.** Drinking vampire blood only heals you. Die within a day with it in your system and you wake after dark, in transition: not a vampire yet, and the sun only hurts your eyes. Drink human blood within 24 game hours (a blood bag, or a person) and it is done. Do nothing and you die the ordinary way: the story goes back to the hour before you died. After that: wood, fire, vervain and the sun hurt you; you need a daylight ring from a witch, who can take the spell back out of it; you cannot enter a home until you are asked in; human blood feeds you fully and animal blood half as well; most of the town drinks vervain, and their blood burns; a wooden bolt stays lodged, and nothing heals, until you pull it out; with no blood left you weaken by stages and desiccate. The humanity switch is a switch.
- **Werewolf.** Once the curse is awake you are stronger and faster than a person and less than a vampire, more as the moon fills and when you are angry. On full-moon nights you turn into a wolf whether you like it or not, and stay one until dawn: a real wolf, with a venomous bite. Chain yourself in the Lockwood cellar before moonrise, or earn a kyanite moonlight ring, which gives you the choice. Wolfsbane is your vervain. Silver does nothing to anyone.
- **Hybrid.** A werewolf who dies with the blood of Corvin, the Original hybrid, in their system wakes in transition and needs the blood of the human doppelgänger to finish it; ordinary blood does nothing. A hybrid has both trees, changes shape at will, walks in the sun, is venomous in either shape, and is burned by vervain and wolfsbane both. You are sired to Corvin and cannot raise a hand to him until a hundred willing changes break the bond.
- **Original.** Late, and by an old spell at Coven Hollow. Only white oak ends you.
- **Human again.** The Cure is twenty floors under Fell's Church. One dose, and whoever takes it can never be turned again.

## The rules everything else obeys

- **Killing a vampire** takes wood through the heart, fire, the sun, the head (a heavy blow with a blade) or the heart torn out. Anything else only downs it: it lies grey for a few seconds and gets back up unless you finish it (E). A hybrid dies only by the head or the heart.
- **An Original** cannot be killed by any of that. Corvin gets up again until a White Oak Stake, carved from the old Wickery Bridge sign, goes through his heart while he is down. That kills every vampire of his line within the hour, which may include you. The ash dagger holds a pure Original as dead while it stays in, kills an ordinary vampire who uses it, and does nothing to the hybrid. There are other ways to end it: a desiccation spell, or a bargain.
- **A werewolf bite** is slow death to a vampire: a day to live, nothing heals, and only Corvin's blood cures it (a Heretic can siphon it out). To an Original it is a fever. To a hybrid, nothing.
- **Compulsion** works on people with no vervain in them, and on nobody else. Only an Original can compel a vampire. The Grill's coffee is vervain.
- **The Council** hunts whatever the town has seen for what it is, with vervain, wolfsbane and wooden rounds. A vampire who kills one of the Five is haunted for a day.

## What is in it

- **Combat.** Three-hit combos that differ by weapon class, charged heavy attacks, a dodge with invulnerability, blocking, timed parries that stagger, ranged weapons, twenty-three active abilities across five skill trees (Hunter, Witch, Werewolf, Vampire, Original), enemy attacks that telegraph on the ground, poise and stagger.
- **Materials matter.** Wood and fire for vampires, white oak for Originals, magic for spirits. Vervain or wolfsbane coats a blade, and each only hurts what it is poison to.
- **Loot.** Six rarities, twenty-five affixes, twelve relics with their own rules, reforging to +10, salvage.
- **Growth without a ceiling.** Levels, four attributes, the trees your nature opens, and Ascension ranks bought with sigils from the Tomb.
- **A county to walk.** The town, the Old Cemetery, the Whispering Woods, Fell Chapel, Coven Hollow, the Falls, Wickery Bridge, Varga Manor, Blackridge Quarry, Abel Farm, Dunham Lake, the Hunters' Camp. Day and night, an eight-day moon, weather, waypoints, respawning chests and herbs. No vampire is out by day without a ring; the Falls pack are people until the full moon.
- **The Tomb.** Procedurally built floors under Fell's Church. The first four are the tomb of 1864, where the vampires a Thorne witch sealed in still lie desiccated. The Warden keeps the fifth, Corvin the tenth, and the Sleeper, with the Cure, the twentieth. It does not end.
- **Quests and bounties.** A main story every bloodline can finish, side quests, and a notice board that posts new work every morning. The Codex (in the menu) fills in with the rules of the town as you learn them.
- **Saving.** Automatic, to the browser (and to your Claude account when played as an artifact). A save code moves a character between devices. Saves from before the bloodlines are brought forward.

## Build

```
pip install bpy
python3 tools/build_models.py   # Blender builds every model: assets/models.glb + assets/models.js
tools/build.sh                  # bundles src/*.js into game.js
```

Source layout: `src/00-core.js` renderer, input, audio, particles · `10-data.js` bloodlines, items, skills, enemies, zones · `20-world.js` map, Tomb generator, sky and moon · `30-actors.js` animation, damage and who it kills, enemy AI · `35-player.js` controls, the wolf body, abilities, feeding · `40-systems.js` stats, species and turning, loot, quests, saving · `45-story.js` people, quests, diary, codex · `50-ui.js` interface · `60-main.js` character creation, loop.

## Tests

Playwright scripts in `tools/tests/` drive the game through `window.HM`. They need a static server on port 8765 serving this directory and are run from another directory, with a folder for screenshots:

```
python3 -m http.server 8765 &
cd /tmp && node /path/to/hollow-moon/tools/tests/origins.js /tmp/out
```

`origins.js` each bloodline through its transformation · `systems.js` quests, combat, menus, the Tomb, turning, saving, old saves · `abilities.js` every active skill, and the town's services · `balance.js` the species side by side, pacing, level 1 fights · `soak.js` every enemy against each kind of player · `screens.js` screenshots. Each prints one line per assertion and exits non-zero if any failed.
